Last updated: May 13, 2026 | Bot: ShyamX (All-in-one Security & Utility)

1. What Data We Process

ShyamX processes message content of all messages sent in channels the app can view to:

  • Provide AI Automod & Automod to filter spam, bad words, links, phishing, toxicity
  • Provide AI Security & Antinuke to detect raids and server nuking
  • Provide Ticket System, Welcome, Auto-role
  • Provide 800+ total commands (90+ slash) and Message Forward & Backup

ShyamX processes server members data to:

  • Ticket - add/remove members to ticket channels
  • Welcome & Auto-role on guildMemberAdd
  • Antinuke & AI Security to detect mass bans/kicks
  • Backup & userinfo functionality

2. How We Use It

We use guild events: messageCreate, guildMemberAdd, guildMemberRemove. Content is scanned in real-time and not logged permanently.

3. Data Storage & Sharing

  • We do NOT store message content permanently.
  • Member data is cached temporarily only for security purposes.
  • We do NOT share any data with any third party.
  • Data is never sold or used for advertising.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Cache auto-clears. You can request deletion via our support server.
